# PRODUCT.md

## Register
product

## Target Users
- First responders and emergency personnel operating in disaster grids.
- Hikers, trekkers, and outdoor individuals in communication-severed zones.
- Founders Fest hackathon judges looking for highly reliable, offline-first tactical dashboards.

## Product Purpose
- Provide absolute communication resiliency over WebRTC and Bluetooth mesh channels when cellular networks crash.
- Deliver situational awareness via localized maps, survivor coordinates, and telemetry index values.
- Facilitate zero-network status broadcasts (Safe Check) and packet sharding (Intel Drop).

## Brand & Personality
- **Tactical**: Dense, precise, mission-critical, monospaced cues, high contrast.
- **Reliable**: Reassuring and immediate feedback; zero unnecessary loading delay.
- **Minimalist Accent**: Dark surfaces, muted tones, and highly focused semantic alerts.

## Anti-References
- Over-decorated SaaS dashboards with soft pastel gradients or floating card-in-card elements.
- Maps used as page-background wallpapers.
- Slower animated UI flows.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
- Strict WCAG 4.5:1 contrast ratios on labels.
- Native system-ui font stacks for fast offline screen rendering.
- Reduced-motion safe state loops.
